FAQ

Questions About Don and His Work

What is AEO - Answer Engine Optimization?
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring content to appear directly in search engine answer boxes, featured snippets, People Also Ask results, and voice search responses. AEO focuses on capturing position zero and giving search engines the exact structured answer format they need to surface your content at the top of results - before organic link #1.
What is GEO - Generative Engine Optimization?
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing content to be cited and referenced by AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and other large language models. GEO ensures your brand and expertise show up when AI systems generate answers for your target audience's questions - even when they never click a link.
What does The SEO Guru specialize in?
Don Phelps - The SEO Guru - specializes in Local SEO, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Google Business Profile optimization, entity-driven SEO, and technical SEO. He helps businesses rank in traditional search while also building visibility in AI-powered search results through the NSYGHT Stack framework.
What is the Local SEO Unlocked podcast?
Local SEO Unlocked is Don Phelps's podcast covering local search strategy, Google Business Profile optimization, AI-powered search trends, and actionable SEO tactics. The show is available on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, and Buzzsprout with 19+ episodes published.
What is the NSYGHT Stack?
The NSYGHT Stack is Don Phelps's framework that combines traditional SEO with GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), and AIO (AI Optimization) to build full-spectrum search visibility. The stack ensures businesses rank in classic search results and also show up when AI tools answer questions about their industry.
