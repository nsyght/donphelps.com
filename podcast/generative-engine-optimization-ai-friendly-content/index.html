From SEO to GEO: Making Your Content AI-Friendly in the Age of Generative Search

Local SEO Unlocked · August 29, 2025 · 16 min listen

This episode explains generative engine optimization (GEO), the work of making content the trusted source AI tools cite when they generate answers. It compares GEO with traditional SEO, walks through the benefits and drawbacks, outlines how to build a GEO strategy, and covers how different AI platforms weigh content and how to measure results.

Key takeaways

  • Shift the goal from earning clicks to being referenced. GEO success is measured by how often AI tools use or cite your content, not only by traffic and conversions.
  • Start with research into how people naturally phrase questions in your field, including conversational long-tail queries and real pain points, and note how AI tools already reference your content.
  • Make content easy to parse with clear headings, bullet points, tables and schema markup, and back it with real expertise, experience, authority and trust signals.
  • Keep the technical basics strong: fast pages, mobile optimization, clean site architecture and solid internal linking all help AI crawlers access and understand your content.
  • Tailor lightly by platform. Perplexity leans toward timely, specialized content, Claude favors detailed and logically structured long-form content without keyword stuffing, and Gemini expects Google best practices at a higher quality bar.
  • Protect your human voice. Write for people in a way AI can understand, avoid bland over-optimized copy, set baseline metrics now and track brand appearances in AI responses over time.

Questions this episode answers

What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the process of optimizing content so it gets featured and cited when AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity generate answers. The goal is to become a trusted source those systems reference, rather than only ranking on a results page.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank pages in a list and earn clicks, measured by traffic and conversions. GEO aims to get your information included inside AI-generated responses, measured by how often your content is used or referenced. GEO expands SEO rather than replacing it.

How do you measure GEO results?

Monitor how your brand appears in AI responses across platforms, watch branded search volume and direct traffic, and look at which content types get cited most. Set imperfect baseline metrics now and track improvements over time, using AI visibility tools alongside Google Search Console and Google Analytics.
