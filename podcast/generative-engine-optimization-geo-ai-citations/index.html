Invisible Online? GEO Is Your New Survival Strategy

Local SEO Unlocked · July 6, 2025 · 17 min listen

This episode explains generative engine optimization, the work of getting content cited, summarized or surfaced inside AI-generated answers rather than only ranking in search results. It covers why zero-click searches make this urgent, then walks through content structure, trust and authority signals, technical foundations, and how this approach builds on traditional SEO rather than replacing it.

Key takeaways

  • Write clear, fluent content with a clean heading hierarchy. Use one main heading, keep subheadings in logical order without skipping levels, and break ideas into short paragraphs.
  • Use question and answer or answer-first formats. Well-structured FAQ sections are linked to showing up more often in AI overviews.
  • Back claims with evidence. Add quotes and data points marked up with proper HTML tags, and link out to credible sources like government sites, academic studies and reputable publishers.
  • Show who is behind the content. Clear author information, published and updated dates, and About, Contact and privacy policy pages all act as trust signals.
  • Build credibility off your site too. A press page or newsroom that links to media coverage, interviews and awards helps, and mentions of your brand elsewhere are a strong signal for inclusion in AI answers.
  • Cover the technical basics: schema markup in JSON-LD checked with Google's Rich Results Test, solid titles and meta descriptions, fast and stable pages, canonical URLs, HTTPS, mobile-friendly design, image alt text and internal links.

Questions this episode answers

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

Generative engine optimization is the practice of getting your content cited, summarized or surfaced directly in AI-generated answers from tools like Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Bing Copilot. Instead of chasing the click, the goal is to be the trusted source the AI uses to build its answer.

Does GEO replace traditional SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO. Traditional SEO handles discoverability, making sure search engines and language models can find your content, while GEO handles selection, making sure the AI chooses, trusts and cites it.

How do I get AI tools like ChatGPT to cite my content?

Make your content easy to understand and easy to trust. Use clear structure and question-based sections, back claims with quotes, data and credible sources, add schema markup, and build mentions of your brand on trusted sites beyond your own.
