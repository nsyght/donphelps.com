LLMS.txt: The Secret Map for Teaching AI to Understand Your Website

Local SEO Unlocked · July 7, 2025 · 14 min listen

This episode explains the llms.txt file, a plain text Markdown file in a site's root directory that hands AI models a curated list of a site's most important pages. It covers why AI tools need that guidance, how to create and upload the file, the benefits, best practices and the mistakes to avoid.

Key takeaways

  • AI tools often fetch content in real time with a short memory and no stored index, and they can stumble on complex HTML or heavy JavaScript. An llms.txt file points them straight to your most valuable pages.
  • Structure each entry as a clean URL with a descriptive title in Markdown, plus an optional short description, and group entries under clear section headings like tutorials, products and about us.
  • Keep the list focused on your most important pages. Leave out login pages, duplicate content, outdated pages and anything sensitive or internal.
  • Name the file exactly llms.txt, place it in the root directory rather than a subfolder, save it with standard text encoding and double-check the Markdown syntax, since small errors can make it unreadable.
  • For most sites, an automated generator, SEO plugin or host feature is safer and faster than writing and updating the file by hand.
  • Test the file by asking ChatGPT or Gemini questions about your site and checking whether the answers reflect your guidance, and update it whenever your content changes.

Questions this episode answers

What is an llms.txt file?

An llms.txt file is a lightweight plain text file written in Markdown that lives in your website's root directory. It gives AI models like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini a curated list of your most important pages so they understand your site better.

How is llms.txt different from robots.txt and sitemap.xml?

Robots.txt sets rules for traditional search engine crawlers, and sitemap.xml lists all of your URLs for search engines. Llms.txt is a hand-picked list of key pages written for AI tools. It complements the other two files and does not replace them.

Do AI models actually use llms.txt?

Not every AI tool honors it yet, but many leading models are beginning to or are expected to. It does not directly affect Google rankings, but setting it up early helps AI represent your brand more accurately.
