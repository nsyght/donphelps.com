Local SEO Unlocked / Episode 11

What Makes Google Choose You?

Local SEO Unlocked · May 21, 2025 · 28 min listen

This episode explains how search engines find, store and rank web pages, moving from crawling and indexing through to the results page. It covers the main groups of ranking factors, the difference between on-page and off-page SEO, why search intent matters, and which popular SEO beliefs are myths. It closes with practical guidance on local visibility, doing SEO yourself versus hiring help, and schema markup.

Key takeaways

  • Make your site easy to crawl and index. Search engines follow links to discover pages, so a clean site structure, logical internal links and solid technical setup help the right pages get found.
  • Match each page to the searcher's intent. Someone looking for the best coffee makers wants comparisons and reviews, not a history lesson, and a page that misses the intent will struggle to rank or keep visitors.
  • Treat title tags and meta descriptions as your ad copy in the results. Keep titles accurate and concise, and write descriptions that give people a reason to click your listing over the others.
  • Structure pages with clear heading tags, readable URLs, compressed images with descriptive alt text, and internal links that show search engines how your content connects.
  • Earn links on quality and relevance, not volume. A few links from respected sites in your industry are worth more than a pile of weak ones, and great content is the most reliable way to attract them.
  • If you serve a local area, claim and fully optimize your Google Business Profile. Keep hours, address, phone and photos accurate, respond to reviews and post updates.

Questions this episode answers

How do search engines decide which websites rank first?

Search engines crawl the web, index what they find, then rank pages against each query using many signals. Key factors include content quality and relevance, the quality of links pointing to the site, technical health like speed and mobile friendliness, and how well the page matches the searcher's intent.

What is the difference between on-page and off-page SEO?

On-page SEO is everything you control on your own site, such as content, titles, headings, images and site structure. Off-page SEO is work done outside your site to build reputation and authority, with earning links from other credible websites as the classic example.

Can I do SEO myself or should I hire someone?

You can do SEO yourself, especially on a smaller site, if you have the time to learn keyword research, on-page optimization and content creation. For larger sites, competitive markets or faster results, an experienced professional or agency often makes more sense because they bring the expertise and tools.
