Backlinks Gone Wrong: The Comedy of Linking Fails

Local SEO Unlocked · July 5, 2025 · 23 min listen

This episode digs into backlinks and why quality and relevance now outweigh sheer volume. It explains topical and geographic relevance, trust flow, authority metrics and natural anchor text, then covers internal linking, how to spot and disavow toxic links, and the trends shaping link building, including niche communities, visual content and social media.

Key takeaways

  • Prioritize relevance. Links from sites in your own niche, and for local businesses from local bloggers or event sites in your area, carry more weight than links from unrelated sources.
  • Look for the combination of trust and relevance. A highly trusted site helps most when it is also connected to your industry.
  • Use authority metrics like Domain Authority as a starting point, not a rule. A slightly less authoritative site that is closely tied to your niche can still be very valuable.
  • Keep anchor text natural and descriptive. Skip generic phrases like "click here" and keyword-stuffed phrases, and describe what the linked page actually offers.
  • Build links through value: guest posts on relevant sites, reputable local directories and business listings, genuine relationships with site owners in your niche, and personalized outreach that offers something in return.
  • Review your backlink profile regularly. Watch for links from unrelated or spammy sites and keyword-stuffed anchors, and use the disavow tool in Google Search Console to ask Google to ignore the bad ones.

Questions this episode answers

What makes a backlink high quality?

A high quality backlink comes from a trustworthy site that is relevant to your industry or local area and uses natural, descriptive anchor text. It should read like a genuine recommendation, not something forced onto the page.

What is trust flow in SEO?

Trust flow is a measure of how much search engines trust a website based on the quality of the sites linking to it. Think of it like a credit score for websites: links from trusted sources raise it, while links from spammy or unrelated sites drag it down.

How do I get rid of toxic backlinks?

Use the disavow tool in Google Search Console. You upload a file listing the links you want Google to ignore when it evaluates your site. Flag links from unrelated sites, sites with low authority or high spam scores, and links with keyword-stuffed anchor text.
