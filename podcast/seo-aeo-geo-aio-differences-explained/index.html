Beyond SEO: Understanding AEO, GEO, and AIO in the AI-Powered Digital World

Local SEO Unlocked · July 6, 2025 · 8 min listen

This episode defines four overlapping strategies: traditional SEO, answer engine optimization (AEO), generative engine optimization (GEO) and AI optimization (AIO). It explains what each one aims for and makes the case that they complement each other rather than compete.

Key takeaways

  • Keep traditional SEO as the foundation. Keywords, quality backlinks and technical work like site speed still drive visibility in standard search results.
  • For AEO, write clear, concise answers to specific questions and use structured data so your content can be extracted as the featured snippet or voice answer.
  • For GEO, aim to be cited inside AI-generated responses from tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity. Those tools generate answers instead of ranking pages, so your content has to read as a trusted, authoritative source.
  • Know the difference: AEO is about being extracted as the direct answer, while GEO is about being cited or included within an answer the AI builds.
  • Use AIO in two ways: lean on AI tools to speed up research, outlining, editing and keyword ideas, and optimize your whole site so chatbots and voice assistants can understand and use it.
  • Run all four together. Relying on traditional SEO alone is no longer enough, because people now find information in results, answer boxes and AI conversations.

Questions this episode answers

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO focuses on getting your content extracted as the direct answer, such as a featured snippet or voice search response. GEO focuses on getting your content cited or included inside a response that an AI tool generates. The two are often used interchangeably, but the goals and tactics differ.

What does AIO mean in digital marketing?

AIO, or AI optimization, has two sides. It can mean using AI tools to create and improve content faster, and it can mean optimizing your content and website so chatbots, voice assistants and other AI tools can understand and use it.

Is traditional SEO still worth doing?

Yes. SEO is still the foundation for showing up in search results. It just is not enough on its own anymore, so it works best alongside AEO, GEO and AIO.
