Making Your Website a Bestseller: SEO Tips That Work

Local SEO Unlocked · March 3, 2025 · 16 min listen

This episode explains SEO by comparing Google to a library and a website to a book on its shelves. It covers on-page basics like keywords, fresh content, site structure, mobile optimization and speed, then moves to off-page work like earning quality backlinks, guest blogging, social media and online reviews.

Key takeaways

  • Use clear, relevant keywords so Google understands what your site is about, but work them in naturally. Keyword stuffing can hurt rankings.
  • Backlink quality beats quantity. A link from a reputable, relevant source such as a university site is worth more than one from a random blog or forum post.
  • Update content regularly. Fresh information signals to Google that your site is active and valuable.
  • Make your site easy to navigate with clear menus, internal links and a logical structure, since Google watches how people interact with pages.
  • Use responsive design so your site works on any screen. Google mainly uses the mobile version of a site for ranking.
  • Promote your content after you publish it through social media, email and outreach to other sites, and encourage reviews on Google and other platforms to build trust.

Questions this episode answers

Does keyword stuffing still work for SEO?

No. Stuffing keywords can actually hurt your rankings. Google looks for quality content that is genuinely relevant, so keywords should be used naturally, as if writing for a person.

What is off-page SEO?

Off-page SEO is everything done outside your website to help it rank, such as earning backlinks, guest blogging, being active on social media and collecting online reviews. It builds your site's reputation and authority alongside on-page work.

Why is mobile optimization important for SEO?

Google uses a mobile-first index, meaning it mainly looks at the mobile version of your site for ranking. A site that does not adjust to phone screens is hard to use and can be penalized.
