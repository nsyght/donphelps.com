Mastering the Art of Branded Search-Why It Matters

Local SEO Unlocked · March 3, 2025 · 18 min listen

This episode explains why ranking for your own brand name is not automatic and how to control what people find when they search for you on Google, Bing or AI chatbots. It covers brand compounds, three audience groups, a four-step optimization process, structured data for AI summaries, and the metrics used to measure results.

Key takeaways

  • Do not assume you will rank for your own name. Branded search covers what appears anywhere someone searches your brand, including AI chatbots.
  • Study brand compounds, your brand name plus words like pricing, careers, reviews or customer service, to understand what searchers actually want.
  • Plan for three audiences: existing customers with post-purchase needs like login or support, prospective buyers comparing options, and neutral information seekers such as journalists or researchers.
  • Follow four steps: identify keywords with Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and third-party tools, sort them by audience, prioritize by intent rather than volume alone, and fix mismatches between searches and landing pages.
  • Handle unwanted keywords like an old brand name or a discontinued product by redirecting those searchers to a relevant current page instead of a dead end.
  • Use structured data to help search engines and AI understand your pages, and track branded search volume, click-through rate, branded traffic, bounce rate, time on site and conversions.

Questions this episode answers

What is branded search?

Branded search is everything that shows up when someone searches for your specific brand name, whether on Google, Bing or an AI chatbot. Optimizing it means making sure people find accurate, relevant information about you at the right moment.

What are brand compounds?

Brand compounds are searches that combine your brand name with extra words, such as pricing, reviews, careers or customer service. They reveal the searcher's intent, whether they are ready to buy, need help or are comparing you to competitors.

How do you measure branded search performance?

Track branded search volume, click-through rate, traffic from branded queries, bounce rate, time on site and conversions. Then analyze the trends: a drop in volume or a low click-through rate is a signal to investigate and adjust titles, descriptions or content.
